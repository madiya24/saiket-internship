5 Tips for Learning to Code

Introduction

Learning to code can feel overwhelming at the start. There are many languages, tools and tutorials, and it is hard to know where to begin. The good news is that a few simple habits make the journey much smoother. Here are five tips that helped me get started.

The 5 Tips

  1. Start with the basics. Learn HTML, CSS and one programming language well before moving on.
  2. Practise every day. Even 30 minutes a day beats a long session once a week.
  3. Build small projects. A quiz, a to-do list or a landing page teaches more than watching videos.
  4. Read other people's code. It shows you new ideas and better ways to solve problems.
  5. Don't fear mistakes. Bugs are part of the process, and fixing them is how you learn.

Conclusion

Nobody becomes a developer overnight. Stay consistent, keep building, and be patient with yourself. Every line of code you write, even the broken ones, moves you forward.
